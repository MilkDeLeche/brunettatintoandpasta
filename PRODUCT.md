# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Guests in McAllen and the Rio Grande Valley choosing a restaurant for dinner, a celebration, or lunch. They need to browse the food, find the location, and reserve or call from a phone as easily as from a desktop.

## Product Purpose

Brunetta Tinto & Pasta is an Italian fine-dining showcase and reservation funnel. The PRD is authoritative: Make a Reservation is primary, View Menu secondary, with Call and Get Directions supporting the visit.

## Brand Personality

Warm, refined, welcoming. Design 1 preserves the approved Amrit Palace reference's dark surfaces, cream typography, amber menu panel, large serif headings, and photographic composition. Design 2 retains its existing light restaurant identity.

## Anti-references

No unrelated Indian restaurant content, multiple locations, invented ratings or testimonials, gift-card commerce, newsletter without a destination, or unsupported private-events claims. Avoid generic placeholder panels where real restaurant photography exists.

## Design Principles

- Preserve each concept's established composition and typography.
- Lead guests toward a table and make the full menu easy to browse.
- Source business facts and menu prices from src/data/brunetta.ts.
- Use the restaurant's supplied imagery with accurate descriptions; keep missing content in SETUP-LIST.md.
- Publish testimonials only from the approved list.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Follow the PRD's WCAG 2.2 AA target: readable contrast, visible keyboard focus, accessible navigation and dialogs, responsive layouts, and reduced-motion support. Multilingual content is outside the PRD's v1 scope.
